Frequently Asked Questions

Is it truly free to post a property on PuneUP?

Yes! Individual homeowners can list their flat or villa completely free with standard visibility. Our paid packages are optional for owners and brokers who want premium search ranking, social media advertising, and concierge escorts.

What is MahaRERA document verification?

Under Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority regulations, our legal associates review your 7/12 extract, Index II, and commencement/occupancy certificate so buyers know your property has clear title papers.

How quickly do I get buyer or tenant responses?

In high-demand zones like Baner, Hinjawadi, and Kharadi, verified listings on Standard Pro and Platinum packages typically receive their first verified inquiries within 24 to 48 hours.
