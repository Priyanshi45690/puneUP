Got Questions?

Pune Real Estate Broker FAQ

Everything you need to know about working with certified property consultants in Pune.

How do I verify an agent's MahaRERA registration number?
Every agent listed on PuneUP carries an official MahaRERA broker license starting with 'A521' (Pune district code). You can instantly verify this license on the official MahaRERA web portal (maharera.mahaonline.gov.in) under the 'Registered Real Estate Agents' tab to inspect certification validity, registered addresses, and disciplinary records.
Do buyers pay brokerage on primary builder flats in Pune?
No. For all primary/under-construction builder projects in Baner, Hinjawadi, Kharadi, and across Pune, buyers pay 0% brokerage. Certified channel partner brokers are compensated directly by developer marketing budgets. Brokerage fees (standard 1% to 2%) apply only to secondary resale transactions or rental lease executions.
Can PuneUP agents assist Non-Resident Indian (NRI) buyers?
Yes. All our senior consultants specialize in NRI real estate transactions, including NRE/NRO rupee remittances, Special Power of Attorney (PoA) registration, remote video walkthroughs, and digital e-registration with Pune sub-registrar offices.
What documents should I request from an agent before buying resale property?
Before committing earnest money, ask for: 1) Mother Deed and chain of title deeds, 2) Encumbrance Certificate (Index II) for past 15-30 years, 3) Society Share Certificate & No Objection Certificate (NOC), 4) Latest Property Tax receipt from Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) or PCMC, and 5) Sanctioned Building Plan and Occupancy Certificate (OC).
